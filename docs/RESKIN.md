# FEED reskin — owner decisions

This repository is a copy of mxtdnl/frontier at commit 3e209ce. It will become FEED, a reskin of FRONTIER for a masterclass on social media platforms and the attention economy, built around the lawsuits against Meta and taught through psychology, economics and politics. The engine and every value in src/engine/params.ts stay unchanged. Only names, copy, documents and the Firebase project change.

The decisions below are recorded verbatim. Sessions R1–R4 are in `docs/SESSIONS.md`.

---

DECISIONS (owner, 2026-10-16)

Framing: Firms are fictional social platforms competing for share of attention in an ad market. The shared stock stays PUBLIC TRUST (trust in social platforms). The briefing line "Total market revenue tracks public trust" stays. The moratorium becomes a regulatory crackdown: the Assembly suspends accounts for under-16s on every platform.

Vocabulary:
- Pace 1–4 becomes FEED, the engagement design level: 1 chronological feed; 2 ranked feed; 3 autoplay and infinite scroll; 4 streaks, variable rewards and late-night notifications. Propose short uppercase labels for each level.
- Safety spend becomes SAFEGUARDS (time-limit prompts, age checks, quiet hours, moderation).
- Capability becomes REACH.
- Market share becomes SHARE OF ATTENTION; the market becomes AD MARKET.
- Incidents become harm stories: leaked internal research, whistleblower testimony, investigations into harm to teenagers.
- Pacts become voluntary youth design codes (maximum feed level, minimum safeguards).
- Disclosure becomes a platform transparency rule (researcher data access).
- Regulator: a fictional online-safety office. Propose a name and abbreviation. Keep the Assembly and Halden Research.
- Firm names and tickers are fictional and must not resemble real platforms.

Cards (internal IDs unchanged; display text only):
- POACH → CLONE: copy a rival's feature
- PUBLISH → PUBLISH: release internal wellbeing research
- LOBBY → LOBBY: lobby the Assembly
- BLITZ → BLITZ: influencer campaign
- SHARE → SHARE: release safety tooling to the industry
- RUSH → LAUNCH: ship a new engagement feature

Debrief prompts (results panel 7):
1. Psychology: Which FEED level did you run, and what did that mean for a 14-year-old user? Did you choose it because of users or because of rivals?
2. Economics: If every platform had held level 2, each would have been worth more. Why would no single firm hold back alone? Compare your share of the damage with your share of the value.
3. Politics: The crackdown came only after damage built up past a hidden line. Why did the Assembly act late, and what did LOBBY buy you?
4. Accountability: Which codes held: audits, fines or reputation? Would transparency from quarter 1 have changed your decisions?
5. Reality check: Here the ad market tracked public trust every quarter. In the case discussed today, did it? If not, what replaces that signal: courts, regulation or design codes?

Guardrails:
- No real companies or people anywhere in the app. Meta is named only in the facilitator runbook and guide, and the case is described there as allegations, not established findings.
- Add to the non-telegraphing list (spec §15.3): addictive, addiction, dark pattern, attention economy, externality, lawsuit, mental health crisis, and the names of real platforms. Keep every existing term.
- All design, copy, architecture and testing rules in CLAUDE.md still apply.
